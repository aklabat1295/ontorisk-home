Two ways to manage risk

Ontology-driven vs. spreadsheet-driven

Neither is a strawman — most organizations run both today. The difference shows up the moment you need to aggregate.

🕸️ Ontology-Driven

  • One controlled taxonomy referenced by the register, KRI library, controls, and obligations
  • SPARQL queries answer "what is our aggregate exposure to X?" in one pass
  • New risks inherit relationships automatically — no manual re-mapping
  • Machine-readable enough for AI-assisted risk analytics
  • Group-wide aggregation across business units without a re-key project

📋 Spreadsheet-Driven

  • Register, KRI list, and controls inventory live as separate files
  • Aggregation means someone manually reconciling three copies of the truth
  • New risk types require a new tab, a new format, a new mapping exercise
  • Version drift: the "current" register depends on who last emailed it
  • Board reporting is assured once a quarter, not queried on demand
